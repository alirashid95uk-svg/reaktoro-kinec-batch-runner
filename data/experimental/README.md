# Jayasekara et al. (2020) digitised validation data

`jayasekara_2020_digitized.csv` contains the measured ICP-OES concentrations from Figure 8 and measured pH values from Figure 9 used by the preliminary FYPR code.

The values were copied without numerical alteration from `Jayasekara_2020_Reaktoro_data_digitized.xlsx` in the supplied preliminary-code archive.

Source paper: D.W. Jayasekara, P.G. Ranjith, W.A.M. Wanniarachchi, T.D. Rathnaweera, D. Van Gent, *CO2-brine-caprock interaction: Reactivity experiments on mudstone caprock of South-west Hub geo-sequestration project*, Journal of Petroleum Science and Engineering 189 (2020) 107011. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.petrol.2020.107011

The digitised values are experimental inputs. They are not generated or adjusted by the batch runner.
